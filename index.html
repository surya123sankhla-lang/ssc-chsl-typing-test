SSC CHSL typing test me candidates ko bilingual typing (Hindi aur English) me proficiency dikhani hoti hai. English typing target 35 WPM aur Hindi typing target 30 WPM hai.

Exam Name SSC CHSL (10+2) - Tier-2 Skill Test
Conducting Body Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Typing Language Hindi & English (Bilingual)
English Speed Target 35 WPM (Qualifying)
Hindi Speed Target 30 WPM (Qualifying)
Practice Durations 2 Minutes, 5 Minutes, 7 Minutes
Minimum Accuracy Required 90%+
Error Penalty 5% deduction per 5 errors
Typing Focus Government correspondence, official orders, general administration

📝 SSC CHSL Skill Test Details

SSC CHSL typing test (Skill Test) un candidates ke liye hota hai jo Data Entry Operator (DEO) ya Lower Division Clerk (LDC) ke liye qualified hote hain. DEO posts ke liye typing test ki jagah Data Entry Speed Test (8000 key depressions per hour) hota hai. LDC aur JSA posts ke liye typing test hota hai - English me 35 WPM ya Hindi me 30 WPM. Candidates ko diye gaye paragraph ko accurately type karna hota hai.

💡 SSC CHSL Typing Tips for Success

SSC CHSL typing test me pehle accuracy maintain karein (90%+). Phir speed ko gradually English me 35 WPM aur Hindi me 30 WPM target tak le jayein. SSC typing paragraphs me government-related words jaise "notification", "circular", "order", "appointment", "transfer", "अधिसूचना", "परिपत्र", "आदेश" aate hain. Daily 20 minute typing practice karein. Backspace ka use kam karein aur typing rhythm maintain karein.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions - SSC CHSL Typing Test

Q1. SSC CHSL typing test me kitni speed chahiye?
Ans: SSC CHSL LDC/JSA posts ke liye English me 35 WPM aur Hindi me 30 WPM typing speed qualifying target hai.
Q2. Kya SSC CHSL typing test bilingual hai?
Ans: Haan, SSC CHSL typing test me candidates Hindi ya English me se koi ek language choose kar sakte hain. Dono languages me alag speed targets hain.
Q3. SSC CHSL typing test me DEO ke liye kya hota hai?
Ans: DEO (Data Entry Operator) posts ke liye typing test nahi, Data Entry Speed Test hota hai - 8000 key depressions per hour.
Q4. SSC CHSL typing test me accuracy kitni chahiye?
Ans: SSC typing test me 90% se upar accuracy target karein. Kam accuracy par disqualification ho sakti hai.
Q5. Kya SSC CHSL typing test free hai?
Ans: Haan, ye bilkul free online SSC CHSL typing practice page hai.
Q6. SSC CHSL typing test me kitne attempts allowed hain?
Ans: SSC CHSL typing test me candidates ko 2 attempts diye jaate hain (one practice, one final). Lekin practice tool me unlimited attempts hain.
